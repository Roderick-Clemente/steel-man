## Note: ANSI stripping fix applied in A2

After the A1 runs above, a bug was found during A2 fixture testing: the
classifier did not strip ANSI color escape codes from pytest output, causing
regex patterns like `assert\s+True` to miss tautological assertions in
colored output. The fix (adding `strip_ansi()`) was applied to
`phase-1/scripts/valid-red.py` and documented in
`phase-1/build-evidence/invalid-red-results.txt`.

The A1 results above were obtained with the pre-fix classifier. The syntax
error and green scenarios do not depend on ANSI-sensitive patterns, so their
results are unchanged. The valid RED acceptance also does not depend on
ANSI-sensitive patterns (it uses a substring match on the assertion phrase,
which appears in the `AssertionError:` line without intervening escape
codes). The fix was verified not to change the A1 results — the valid RED
is still accepted after the fix.
